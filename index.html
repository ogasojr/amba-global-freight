About Amba

Your logistics partner from Kenya to the world.

Amba Global Freight Limited is a Kenyan freight forwarding company established in 2008. We support international loose cargo, full containers, customs clearance, transport and general logistics services.

Our mission is to provide personal, efficient service with close attention to detail, competitive pricing and practical guidance through changing shipping and customs requirements.
