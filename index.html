About Amba

Your trusted logistics partner in Kenya and beyond.

Amba Global Freight Limited was incorporated in Kenya in 2008 with the aim of providing international freight forwarding services regionally and internationally.

We handle international loose cargo, full containers, customs clearance, transport and general logistics services in Kenya and across global markets.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional and personal service, with attention to detail and competitive pricing, while helping clients navigate changing regulations, international transportation challenges and logistical complexities.
